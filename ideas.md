## CEO scandal / sudden CEO exit (Kai, 2026-11-12)

Trigger: Nvidia, Jensen Huang resigned 2026-11-12 after an affair/espionage scandal. Idea: category `ceo-exit-scandal`, asset = the company's own stock, compared with its sector index (not the raw price). Questions it could answer: how often was the stock higher 20/60/250 days later, and how deep did the fall go before the low (drawdown range), each with n, 95% CI and range. Needs a free daily stock-price source (not EIA) and a hand list of maybe 20-40 cases (HP 2010, Uber 2017, Intel 2018, Wynn 2018, CBS 2018, McDonald's 2019, ...). Small n, very different companies. The output stays a base rate, never "buy now".
